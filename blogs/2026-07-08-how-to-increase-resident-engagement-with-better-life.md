Keeping residents consistently engaged is one of the biggest challenges in senior living. By combining meaningful life enrichment content with connected technology, communities can increase participation, strengthen connections, and create experiences residents look forward to. This blog shares practical strategies to turn everyday programming into more consistent engagement.
