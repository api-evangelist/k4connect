Artificial intelligence is transforming senior living, but its success depends on a strong data foundation. Without effective data governance, AI can create more risk than value, leading to inaccurate insights, inefficiencies, and compliance concerns. By prioritizing data quality, integration, and accountability, organizations can build trust, improve operations, and unlock the full potential of AI-driven innovation.
